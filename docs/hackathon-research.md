## Published rubric and build rules

The [official guide](https://iqoo.reskilll.com/guide) publishes:

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence owner |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Product quality | 30% | Jury |
| Novelty and impact | 20% | Jury |
| Creative phone use | 15% | HackTracker |
| Technical depth | 15% | Jury |
| Office Kit use | 10% | HackTracker |
| Demo and presentation | 10% | Jury |

The pitch is 3–5 minutes. Entries must run and be demonstrated on iQOO hardware. Teams have 1–3 participants and cannot mix student/professional categories. Native Android, Flutter, React Native and PWA are accepted. Local/open-source models are encouraged; Snapdragon NPU inference is the stated target. The guide does not specify a mandatory NPU runtime or model parameter count.

Its exact build constraint is: **“Original work only: code written during the event window. No shipping a pre-built product.”** Open-source dependencies require attribution. Submit repository and demo assets through Reskilll before its cutoff. HackTracker measures counts/durations; the guide excludes keystrokes, screenshots and browsing from that collection. Loaners remain event property and must be returned.

## Terms that affect preparation

The [Terms & Conditions](https://iqoo.reskilll.com/terms), last updated 10 September 2026, explicitly cover both city rounds and the Finale. Section 03 permits pre-event idea drafting but requires original team submissions created during the event. Third-party APIs and open-source dependencies require licence compliance and attribution. Section 05 prohibits modifying or unlocking loaner hardware. Section 04 preserves participant IP while licensing event review/showcase; separate agreement is needed for commercial exploitation. Section 06 allows organisers to change evaluation rules. Treat the published rubric as the current planning baseline, subject to event updates.

**Planning implication:** research, architecture, environment verification and draft ideas can proceed now. Keep preparation experiments clearly labelled and dated. Obtain explicit organiser guidance before carrying pre-event application code into a competition submission; do not present today's code as event-written work. This is an interpretation of the rules, not an invented ban on research or setup.

## Evidence plan for our assistant

1. Lead with one repeatable local workflow: detect a configured distraction budget, explain the trigger, offer a productive action, and execute a user-approved phone command.
2. Record model identity, licence, runtime, backend and measured latency. Claim Snapdragon NPU execution only after backend/profiling evidence on the actual iQOO device. A working CPU fallback remains accurately labelled.
3. Demonstrate voice input, local focus state, a real alarm intent and a visible decision trace. Pairing with Office Kit is a separate verification item from ADB.
4. Use simulated commitment points for the financial penalty idea in the first demo; a real payment workflow is outside the present architecture decision.
5. Prepare a 3–5 minute pitch plus a shorter backup recording, then adapt export/upload requirements to the authenticated dashboard.
